Who We Are

Techprolabz is a pioneer in the field of Robotics. We are an organisation that invests in creating new innovations and executing them to bring out new technology. Today the world is craving for new "Kalams" and Einsteins, and Techprolabz is trying hard to fill that gap.

We act as a bridge to provide kids the platform that will make themselves land in between top tech-lovers. Techprolabz is working for the upbringing of educational technology in schools and we are very passionate about setting up makerspaces across the country.

Techprolabz doesn't only provide the best platform to showcase and enhance your skills, but it also has an R&D center, where you can work on live projects.
